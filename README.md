# AI Media Company

An experiment: a small media company where every role is filled by an AI agent, with a human owner setting direction and approving final work.

## Why

I come from 20+ years in broadcast and AV engineering. I want to find out, in practice, what an agent-run media company can and can't do, and share what I learn so others can reuse it.

## The team

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| [Marketing](agents/marketing.md) | Content calendar, campaign copy, captions, performance review |
| [Video Editor](agents/video-editor.md) | Scripts, shot lists, edit plans, subtitles, automation scripts |
| [HR](agents/hr.md) | Role definitions, onboarding material, performance reviews of the other agents |

The human owner assigns tasks and approves anything that goes public.
